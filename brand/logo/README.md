# BorneoStack logo

Three stacked layers; the base layer is always the strongest.

| File | Use on | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `borneostack-mark-blue.svg` | White and light backgrounds | Main logo |
| `borneostack-mark-blue-on-dark.svg` | Dark backgrounds, where the brand leads (site header) | Main logo, dark version |
| `borneostack-mark-white.svg` | Black and dark backgrounds (site footer) | Secondary logo |
| `borneostack-mark-black.svg` | White backgrounds, one-colour print, stamps | Secondary logo |

The favicon (`site/public/mark.svg`) is blue on a light browser theme and switches to the white mark on a dark one.
Wordmark: "BorneoStack" in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, tight tracking, in white (dark) or near-black (light).

`mark.svg`, `mark-mono.svg` and `mark-reverse.svg` are the retired green marks from the earlier site.
