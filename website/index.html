Six promises, written into every scope

We are a new studio, so we do not ask you to trust reviews. We put our terms on paper instead, including the ones that protect us both.

  • Scope in writing first

    A one-page scope with deliverables, exclusions, price, date, payment terms and who owns what. Nothing starts until you sign it.

  • The price does not move

    If the work takes us longer than planned, that is our cost. You pay the price in the scope.

  • Running costs shown upfront

    AI usage, hosting and app subscriptions are listed in the scope. You pay those providers directly, never through a markup.

  • Your data stays yours

    Your data and accounts always belong to you. You get a permanent licence to use the system we build for your business.

  • 30 days of free fixes

    If something we built does not work as the scope describes within 30 days of handover, we fix it at no cost.

  • Your information stays private

    We use your documents only for your project and handle personal data under Malaysia’s PDPA 2010.
