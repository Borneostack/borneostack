Foundational Research Pillars

01 / OBSERVATION

Multi-Sensor Earth Observation

Sentinel-1 SAR + Sentinel-2 Multispectral + Local Weather Telemetry

Tropical monsoon cloud cover routinely blinds standard optical satellite sensors. We combine European Space Agency Sentinel-1 C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) with Sentinel-2 10m multispectral channels and in-situ hydrometeorological IoT sensor streams.

02 / CAUSALITY

Causal Early-Warning Models

Separating anthropogenic deforestation from monsoon flood dynamics

Standard deep neural networks confuse heavy seasonal rainfall with human-driven land degradation. Our Structural Causal Models (SCMs) use Pearlian do-calculus and counterfactual estimators to rigorously decouple logging and palm oil concession encroachment from baseline hydrological runoff.

03 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Automated Scientific Reasoning

Verifiable code execution & literature-grounded geospatial feature engineering

We employ language model agents grounded in scientific literature to discover non-linear spatiotemporal features. Every generated kernel undergoes programmatic Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) inspection, proving zero spatial lookahead leakage and deterministic reproducibility across catchment benchmarks.

SCENARIO CAUSAL ANALYSIS: Pristine Dipterocarp Forest Reserve

Under intact primary forest cover, continuous evapotranspiration and dense root structure mitigate peak catchment runoff. Even under high moisture conditions, soil absorption remains high and flood risk is bounded within normal natural thresholds.

Active Research Programs

STAGE 4 BENCHMARK PRG-01

BorneoGeoCausal

Causal Attribution of Catchment Hydrology & Concession Runoff

Decoupling commercial logging and oil palm expansion from monsoon flash flooding across the Kinabatangan and Rajang river basins. Fuses 12-day Sentinel-1 SAR orbital passes with hydrological river gauge networks to compute causal counterfactual damage estimates.

Spatiotemporal SCM Sentinel-1 SAR Kinabatangan Basin Counterfactual Inference
CONTINUOUS SYNTHESIS PRG-02

Agentic Feature Engineering

AST-Verified Geospatial Representation Synthesis

Autonomous reasoning agents iteratively formulate and evaluate novel multispectral vegetation interaction kernels. All generated code undergoes deterministic Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) validation to enforce zero future lookahead, zero spatial cross-contamination, and strict reproducibility.

Biodiversity Shift Forecasting

Canopy Microclimate Buffering & Fragmented Corridors

Principal Investigator & Lab Profile

Station Coordinates: 5.9804° N, 116.0735° E

Dale Jeffrey is the Principal Investigator at BorneoStack Research, based in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. His work is situated at the intersection of spatiotemporal machine learning, causal inference for Earth observation, and verifiable autonomous reasoning systems for tropical ecosystem resilience.

By fusing high-frequency synthetic aperture radar (SAR), cloud-penetrating microwave telemetry, multispectral orbital constellations, and in-situ hydrometeorological IoT streams, his lab constructs open-source causal early-warning architectures. These systems disentangle anthropogenic land-cover changes from extreme monsoon dynamics to safeguard critical rainforest watersheds and endemic biodiversity across Southeast Asia.
