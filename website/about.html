A small studio that builds systems you keep

BorneoStack Technology (BorneoStack) is based in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. We build automations, document assistants and dashboards for businesses in Malaysia and abroad.

Why we exist

Many teams lose hours every week copying numbers between spreadsheets, chasing updates and searching folders for one PDF. A small, well-built system fixes that for good.

Large agencies quote months and open-ended hours for this work. We do the opposite: one focused system, one fixed price in writing, delivered in days or weeks, and looked after once it is live.

We take one project at a time, so the person who builds your system is the person who answers you.

Mission

We build small, reliable systems that save businesses hours every week, at a fixed price agreed before work starts.

Vision

To be the first studio businesses in Borneo call when their work needs automating.
