Questions & answers

Common questions before we start

Anything else you want to clarify? Email dale.jeffrey@borneostack.com directly.

How do fixed-price projects work?
After a free 30-minute discovery call, we send a one-page written scope listing exact deliverables, timeline, and a locked fixed fee. You never pay for unexpected extra hours if something takes us longer to engineer.
Who owns the source code and data?
You do, 100%. Upon handover, all scripts, repositories, workflows, and credentials live in your company’s accounts (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google Workspace, or your own servers) with zero lock-in.
Can Malaysian businesses pay in Ringgit (MYR)?
Yes. Prices are shown in US dollars for international clarity, and Malaysian clients are billed in Ringgit Malaysia (MYR) via local bank transfer at the prevailing rate.
Can you connect with our existing tools like WhatsApp, Google Sheets, or SQL?
Yes. Most clients don’t want a brand-new platform to train staff on—they want their existing spreadsheets, email, WhatsApp/Telegram, accounting software, or databases connected cleanly.
What happens after handover if something breaks?
Every project includes 30 days of complimentary bug-fix coverage and a recorded video walkthrough. If you want ongoing monitoring, updates, or new features later, an optional monthly care plan is available.
How soon can we start?
Usually within 2 to 4 working days after agreeing on the one-page scope. Starter packages often ship within the same week.

Not sure which package fits?

Describe what is slowing your team down in a few lines. We reply within 24–48 hours with a straight recommendation.
